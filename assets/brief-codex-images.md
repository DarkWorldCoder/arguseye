# Image brief for Arguseye (for Codex / ChatGPT image generation)

Product: Arguseye, "eyes for coding agents". It renders a React/Next/Vue component exactly as the app shows it, with no dev server, so an AI coding agent can see what it just built. Brand colours: deep navy #0f172a / #1e293b, white #f8fafc, orange-amber iris #f97316 → #fbbf24, slate grey #94a3b8.

Style: the same soft clay / claymation look as the MarketMirror hero (reference image attached): matte clay materials, soft studio lighting, gentle depth of field, rounded forms, no gloss, no photorealism. Dark navy background unless stated. **No text, letters, logos or UI copy inside the image**; captions and wordmarks are added later in SVG/HTML.

Generate these three, each saved to `assets/generated/`:

1. `hero-argus.png`, 1536×1024 (3:2). A friendly clay Argus: a rounded creature or figure with several large orange-iris eyes, calmly looking at a floating stack of small UI cards (a product card, a navbar strip, a button), each card a thin clay slab with soft shadows. The eyes should feel attentive and kind, not creepy. Composition leaves the left 40% quieter for a headline overlay.

2. `mascot-argus.png`, 1024×1024 (1:1), transparent or plain navy background. The Argus creature alone, front-facing, three to five eyes, compact and iconic enough to read at 64 px. Matches the SVG icon's idea: an eye between two angle brackets `< >` can appear as subtle clay shapes behind it (no literal characters).

3. `social-backdrop.png`, 1280×640 (2:1). A wide, quiet composition: a single clay eye on the right third looking toward the left, a few blurred clay UI cards drifting, lots of dark negative space on the left for the wordmark and tagline.

Also useful if cheap: `empty-state.png`, 800×600, the creature peeking from behind a blank clay card (used in the VS Code panel when nothing is open).
